Our Story

A Kolkata manufacturer built around one idea: get the little things right.

First Touch Apparels is a manufacturer of kids wear, t-shirts and baba suits, operating out of Cossipore in Kolkata, West Bengal. We've been a registered, GST-compliant manufacturing business since 2017 — one product category, done with care, rather than everything done in a hurry.

As a proprietor-led manufacturing unit, we keep our operation close-knit on purpose. Every enquiry is handled directly, every order gets a real conversation about fabric, sizing and finish, and every batch leaves our unit the way we'd want it to arrive for our own kids.

From everyday t-shirts to the traditional baba suit — the dhoti-kurta set worn by little boys at Annaprashan, Poila Boishakh and family celebrations — we manufacture clothing that respects both comfort and culture.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp — we reply personally.

What products does First Touch Apparels manufacture?+
We manufacture kids t-shirts, general kids wear, and traditional baba suits (dhoti-kurta sets) for young boys.
Where is First Touch Apparels located?+
Our manufacturing unit is based in Cossipore, Kolkata, West Bengal, India.
Do you sell to individual customers, or only to businesses?+
We primarily supply retailers, boutiques, wholesalers and distributors. If you're an individual with a specific requirement, reach out and we'll let you know how we can help.
What is a "baba suit"?+
A baba suit is a traditional dhoti-kurta set worn by young boys in Bengali culture, commonly for occasions like Annaprashan, Poila Boishakh and pujas.
Can I place a bulk or wholesale order?+
Yes — bulk and wholesale orders are welcome. Share your requirement over WhatsApp or the form below and we'll take it from there.
Is there a minimum order quantity (MOQ)?+
MOQ can vary by product and design. Contact us with your requirement and we'll confirm the exact minimum for your order.
Can I get custom designs, colours or fabrics?+
We're happy to discuss customisation where feasible. Share your requirements and we'll let you know what's possible for your order size.
What age and size ranges do you manufacture for?+
We manufacture across a range of children's sizes. Tell us the age group you're stocking for and we'll confirm availability.
How do I place an order?+
Call us, message us on WhatsApp, or fill out the enquiry form on this page. We'll discuss your requirement and guide you through the process.
What payment methods do you accept?+
Payment terms are confirmed directly with each buyer at the time of order. Get in touch and we'll share the details.
Do you ship outside Kolkata or West Bengal?+
Reach out with your delivery location and order size, and we'll confirm whether we can dispatch to you.
How long does production take?+
Timelines depend on the products and quantity ordered. We'll give you a clear estimate once we understand your requirement.
Can I visit your facility in Cossipore?+
Yes, visits can be arranged — please call or WhatsApp ahead so we can plan for your visit.
Do you provide samples before a bulk order?+
Speak with us about your order and we'll discuss sample options where applicable.
Is First Touch Apparels GST registered?+
Yes, we've been a GST-registered manufacturing business since 2017.
Are baba suits available only for festive seasons?+
Baba suits are popular around festive and ceremonial occasions, but enquiries are welcome year-round.
How can I contact you for an urgent enquiry?+
The fastest way to reach us is WhatsApp or a direct phone call — both are available at the top and bottom of this page.
